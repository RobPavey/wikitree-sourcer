Introduction:
The Fern Hill Cemetery index lists individuals interred at the Fern Hill Cemetery in Aberdeen, Grays Harbor County, Washington. The index includes the name, burial or cremation location, funeral home, and burial or cremation year. Burial location is arranged by lot/block/grave/section or block/grave/section when only three numbers are listed. The earliest burial year listed is 1877.

There are 33,677 individuals listed in this index.

This index is in English.

This index is open for research.
