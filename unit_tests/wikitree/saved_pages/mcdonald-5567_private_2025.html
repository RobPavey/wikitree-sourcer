      David Tennant is a Scottish actor. He rose to fame for his role as the tenth incarnation of the Doctor in the BBC sci-fi series Doctor Who (2005–2010; 2013), returning to the show as the fourteenth incarnation of the character in 2022.

      Biography [edit]

      David (McDonald) Tennant was born in Scotland.

      David John Tennant (born McDonald) was born on 18 April 1971 in Bathgate, West Lothian, Scotland. His parents were Alexander McDonald and Helen McLeod. His father was the minister for Ralston, Renfrewshire, Scotland, and acted as the Moderator of the General Assembly of the Church of Scotland.

      His maternal great grandparents (William Blair and Agnes (Christie) Blair) were Northern Irish Protestants from County Londonderry, Northern Ireland. They and others signed the Ulster Covenant in 1912, William also being a member of the Orange Order. [1] Their son-in-law, Archie McLeod was a professional footballer, playing for Derry City Football Club.

      The family lived in Ralston, Renfrewshire, Scotland. David was the youngest of three children.[2] [3]

      From a young age, Tennant was passionate about acting, particularly inspired by the television series Doctor Who. He attended Ralston Primary School and Paisley Grammar School, and at the age of 16, he was one of the youngest students to be admitted to the Royal Conservatoire of Scotland in Glasgow, graduating in 1991. He changed his surname to "Tennant" after discovering there was another actor named David McDonald already registered with the actors' union, Equity. He chose the name after Neil Tennant, the lead singer of the Pet Shop Boys.[4] Tennant was later made Honorary Doctor of Drama at the Royal Conservatoire of Scotland.[5]

      Career[edit]

      After graduating, Tennant's career flourished on stage and screen. He joined the Royal Shakespeare Company (RSC), starring in numerous productions, and was nominated for an Olivier Award in 2003 for best actor in Kenneth Lonergan’s Lobby Hero. Significant early TV and film work included the BBC's Casanova mini series (lead role), and parts in Stephen Fry's Bright Young Things and, in 2005 Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire.[2][6]

      This was the year he was cast in and gained significant recognition for his role as the tenth incarnation of the Doctor in the BBC's Doctor Who, a role he held until 2010, and reprised for the show's 50th anniversary in 2013 and again in 2023 as the fourteenth Doctor.[4]

      Polls consistently name Tennant as people's favourite Doctor.[7]

      His other notable roles are too numerous to cover here, but include Amazon's Good Omens and three ITV series; Detective Inspector Alec Hardy in Broadchurch opposite Olivia Colman; the Polonium poisoned Russian spy in Litvinenko, and serial killer Dennis Nielson in Des, for which he won an International Emmy award. His repertoire also includes voice acting roles in How to Train Your Dragon and Star Wars series Ashoka and Clone Wars. All the time Tennant has not forgotten his theatre roots, appearing in countless productions with the RSC and other companies.[2][6]

      All the time whilst making films and tv shows, Tennant never forgot his first love of the theatre, starring in many highly acclaimed productions with the RSC and others.[6]

      Personal Life[edit]

      Tennant married actress Georgia Moffett in 2011. They have five children together, including a son from Georgia's previous relationship, whom David has adopted. Wife Georgia is the daughter of fifth Doctor Peter Davison and actress Sandra Dickinson.[4]

      Social and Political[edit]

      Tennant is a self-proclaimed liberal and socialist who actively supports the Labour Party. He has consistently used his public platform to champion LGBTQ+ rights, most recently championing transgender and non-binary rights through public statements and symbolic gestures. His consistent allyship was formally recognised when he received the LGBT+ Celebrity Ally Award at the 2024 British LGBT+ Awards.[8]
